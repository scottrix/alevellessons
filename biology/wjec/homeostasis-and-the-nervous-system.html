Biology — Homeostasis and the Nervous System

Exam board: WJEC  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

WJEC specification; mainly taken in Wales.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain homeostasis
  • Describe the nervous system
  • Understand hormonal control

Key Points

  • Negative feedback maintains balance
  • Neurones transmit impulses
  • Hormones travel in the blood

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of homeostasis and the nervous system with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: What controls blood glucose?. Solution: Insulin (and glucagon)
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Define homeostasis
  • Name the parts of a neurone
  • What is the role of adrenaline?

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
