Biology — Ecology and Ecosystems

Exam board: Edexcel  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Edexcel (Pearson) specification; check the specific content list for your paper.

Learning Objectives

  • Describe energy flow through ecosystems
  • Understand nutrient cycles
  • Explain population dynamics

Key Points

  • Trophic levels and food chains
  • Carbon and nitrogen cycles
  • Carrying capacity

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of ecology and ecosystems with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: What happens to energy at each trophic level?. Solution: Some is lost as heat, so less is available
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Name two greenhouse gases
  • Describe the carbon cycle in three steps
  • Define a trophic level

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
