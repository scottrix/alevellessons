Business Studies — Strategy and the External Environment

Exam board: AQA  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

AQA specification; content aligns with the national A-Level syllabus.

Learning Objectives

  • Use SWOT and PESTEL
  • Analyse competition
  • Evaluate growth strategies

Key Points

  • SWOT and PESTEL analysis
  • Competitive advantage
  • Organic vs inorganic growth

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of strategy and the external environment with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: What does SWOT stand for?. Solution: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Build a SWOT for a firm
  • Explain a PESTEL factor
  • Compare organic and inorganic growth

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
