Further Mathematics — Polar Coordinates

Exam board: OCR  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Frequently paired with the 'MEI' specification; strong applied component.

Learning Objectives

  • Convert between polar and Cartesian
  • Sketch simple polar curves
  • Find areas in polar form

Key Points

  • x = r cosθ, y = r sinθ
  • r² = x² + y²
  • Area = ½∫r² dθ

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of polar coordinates with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: Convert r = 2 to Cartesian. Solution: x² + y² = 4 (a circle)
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Convert (2, π/3) to Cartesian
  • Sketch r = 2 cosθ
  • Area enclosed by r = 3 from 0 to π

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
