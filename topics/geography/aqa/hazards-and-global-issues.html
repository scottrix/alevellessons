Geography — Hazards and Global Issues

Exam board: AQA  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

AQA specification; content aligns with the national A-Level syllabus.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain natural hazards
  • Analyse climate change impacts
  • Evaluate management strategies

Key Points

  • Tectonic and climatic hazards
  • Vulnerability and resilience
  • Adaptation and mitigation

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of hazards and global issues with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: What is mitigation in climate change?. Solution: Actions to reduce the causes, e.g. cutting emissions
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Classify a hazard as tectonic or climatic
  • Explain a climate change impact
  • Evaluate one mitigation strategy

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
