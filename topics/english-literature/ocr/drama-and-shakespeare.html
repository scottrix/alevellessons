English Literature — Drama and Shakespeare

Exam board: OCR  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Distinct set texts and a strong comparative element across periods.

Learning Objectives

  • Analyse dramatic techniques
  • Interpret Shakespearean language
  • Evaluate staging and performance

Key Points

  • Soliloquy, aside, dramatic irony
  • Iambic pentameter, blank verse
  • Stage directions and staging choices

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of drama and shakespeare with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: Why does Shakespeare use soliloquy?. Solution: To reveal a character's private thoughts directly to the audience
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Explain the effect of dramatic irony
  • Analyse a key soliloquy
  • Discuss how staging conveys theme

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
