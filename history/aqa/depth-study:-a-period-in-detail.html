History — Depth Study: a Period in Detail

Exam board: AQA  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Range of British and world depth/thematic options; source-based paper.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain detailed chronology
  • Analyse key figures
  • Assess significance

Key Points

  • Chronology of events
  • Key individuals and their impact
  • Short-term vs long-term significance

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of depth study: a period in detail with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: How significant was a single ruler?. Solution: Assess personal impact against wider structural forces
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Give a timeline of 5 key events
  • Evaluate one leader's role
  • Weigh short vs long-term significance

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
