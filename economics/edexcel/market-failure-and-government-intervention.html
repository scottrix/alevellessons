Economics — Market Failure and Government Intervention

Exam board: Edexcel  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Edexcel (Pearson) specification; check the specific content list for your paper.

Learning Objectives

  • Identify types of market failure
  • Explain externalities
  • Evaluate government policies

Key Points

  • Public goods, externalities, monopoly
  • Negative and positive externalities
  • Taxes, subsidies, regulation

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of market failure and government intervention with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: What is a negative externality?. Solution: A cost to a third party, e.g. pollution
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Give an example of a public good
  • Explain how a tax corrects pollution
  • Evaluate a subsidy

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
