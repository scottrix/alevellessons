Economics — Macroeconomics

Exam board: OCR  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

OCR specification; note any options relevant to your centre.

Learning Objectives

  • Measure economic growth
  • Explain inflation and unemployment
  • Use fiscal and monetary policy

Key Points

  • GDP as a measure of output
  • Inflation = sustained price rise
  • Fiscal (tax/spending) and monetary (interest rates) policy

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of macroeconomics with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: Name a cost of inflation. Solution: Reduced purchasing power / uncertainty
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Define GDP
  • Explain why unemployment matters
  • Give an example of fiscal policy

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
