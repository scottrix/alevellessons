Further Mathematics — Vectors

Exam board: WJEC  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Pure plus applied options; check the specific pathway chosen.

Learning Objectives

  • Use vector arithmetic and notation
  • Compute scalar and vector products
  • Work with lines in 3D

Key Points

  • Position vectors r = a + λb
  • Dot product for angle cosθ = (a·b)/(|a||b|)
  • Scalar product of perpendicular vectors is zero

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of vectors with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: Find cosθ between (1,2) and (3,4). Solution: (1·3+2·4)/(√5·5) = 11/(5√5)
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Dot (2,−1,3) with (1,0,2)
  • Are (1,2) and (−4,2) perpendicular?
  • Write the line through two points in vector form

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
