Chemistry — Atomic Structure and Bonding

Exam board: CCEA  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

CCEA specification; taken in Northern Ireland.

Learning Objectives

  • Describe atomic structure
  • Explain ionic and covalent bonding
  • Use electronegativity

Key Points

  • Protons, neutrons, electrons
  • Ionic vs covalent bonds
  • Electronegativity trends across the period

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of atomic structure and bonding with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: Number of protons in carbon-14?. Solution: 6 (atomic number is 6)
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • State the charge of a neutron
  • Give an example of an ionic compound
  • Which element is most electronegative?

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
