Physics — Thermal Physics

Exam board: Edexcel  |  Back to subjects

Board-Specific Note

Edexcel (Pearson) specification; check the specific content list for your paper.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain temperature and heat
  • Use specific heat capacity
  • Apply the gas laws

Key Points

  • Q = mcΔθ
  • Ideal gas PV = nRT
  • Kinetic theory and absolute zero

Lesson Plan (50 minutes)

  1. Starter (5 min): Recall prior knowledge of thermal physics with quick questions.
  2. Teaching (15 min): Work through each of the learning objectives, explaining principles step by step.
  3. Key points review (5 min): Revisit the key points together, confirming understanding.
  4. Worked example (10 min): Model the example question: Heat to raise 2 kg of water by 10 K (c=4200 J/kgK)?. Solution: Q = 2 × 4200 × 10 = 84 kJ
  5. Practice (10 min): Students attempt the practice questions independently; circulate and support.
  6. Plenary (5 min): Review answers and address misconceptions.

Homework

  • Heat to melt 0.5 kg of ice (specific latent heat 334 kJ/kg)
  • State units of R
  • Explain temperature in kinetic terms

Assessment

Check practice answers against the model answer; use the built-in practice questions as formative assessment.
